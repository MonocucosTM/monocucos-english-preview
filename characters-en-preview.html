The MONOCUCOS universe

MEET THE MONOCUCOS

A community where we are all different. And we all belong.

Characters of different ages, personalities, and ways of seeing the world who accompany children on their adventures.

A community where everyone has something to contribute.

In MONOCUCOS, every character has a unique way of seeing the world. Some guide, some protect, some ask questions, some play, and some are discovering who they are.

Together, they form a community where differences do not divide us: they help us build new connections.
