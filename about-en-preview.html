ABOUT US

A world for everyone,
where we are all one.

MONOCUCOS was born from a simple idea: to help children discover who they are, understand others, and learn to live together.

We believe education can help us build a more compassionate society.

WHAT IS MONOCUCOS

Much more than characters.

MONOCUCOS is a universe of characters, stories, and experiences that supports children in their emotional and social learning. Through imagination, we open conversations about differences, our feelings, and the ways we connect with others.

Inclusion

Creating spaces where everyone can belong.

Empathy

Understanding what others feel and experience.

Diversity

Learning to recognize and value what makes us different.

Community

Understanding that together, we can make a difference.

MONOCUCOS

MONOCUCOS emerged from the meeting of different cultures, experiences, and ways of understanding the world. Its original creator, Alejandra Monterrubio, and John Núñez bring artistic and professional backgrounds shaped by multiculturalism, communication, and creativity.

Alejandra Monterrubio

Mexican illustrator, graphic designer, and artist.

Her experience living in Canada, in a society known for its cultural diversity, was one of the inspirations behind the creation of MONOCUCOS.

Her artistic and multicultural perspective helps shape a visual universe where differences, colors, and imagination become a language that introduces children to acceptance and living together.

John Núñez

Colombian writer and communications professional.

His career has spanned advertising agencies in Latin America and Europe, working in content creation, creative direction, and communication strategy development.

His professional experience and passion for writing bring a narrative and cultural perspective to MONOCUCOS, communicating values through stories that encourage reflection on diversity, tolerance, and respect.

A GROWING PROJECT

From stories to experiences.

MONOCUCOS stories can become tools for conversation, play, imagination, and exploring emotions and values in different settings.

That is why the project reaches schools, families, communities, and institutions, creating new ways to support children.

The world changes when we learn to see one another differently.

MONOCUCOS aims to be part of that change, one story, one conversation, and one experience at a time.
